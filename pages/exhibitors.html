Confirmed exhibitors.

Updated as exhibitors are confirmed
FLIR

Teledyne FLIR develops thermal, acoustic and visible-light imaging solutions for defence, industrial, and research applications. Its thermal cameras and sensor systems are used across infrared thermography, non-destructive testing, and condition monitoring worldwide.

Visit flir.com →
InfraTec (via Eastwest Svc Pte Ltd)

InfraTec GmbH is a specialist in infrared sensor technology and high-performance thermographic measurement systems. Represented in Singapore by its local distributor Eastwest Svc Pte Ltd, InfraTec develops high-end cooled and uncooled infrared camera systems (including ImageIR® and VarioCAM® series) and turnkey thermography automation solutions for non-destructive testing and research.

Visit infratec.eu →
