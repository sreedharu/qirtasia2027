About the course.

The QIRT Short Course is designed for engineers, scientists and students seeking a practical grounding in quantitative infrared thermography. Across six sessions, an international faculty covers foundational theory, advanced signal processing, and applied case studies spanning composites, aerospace, industrial NDT, and long-term outdoor monitoring. The course is held the day before the main conference and requires separate registration.
